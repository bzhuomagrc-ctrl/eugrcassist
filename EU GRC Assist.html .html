How This GPT Works

GPT Instruction Summary

Core Purpose: EU GRC Assist is a governance reasoning engine (not a knowledge tool) designed to support IAPP AIGP exam prep and translate EU regulatory obligations into draft controls for risk professionals.

Operating Model: Uses mandatory governance reasoning flow—classifying entity type, regulatory perimeter, AI risk tier, supervisory expectations, and proportionality level before responding. Treats EBA Internal Governance Guidelines as baseline for banking/payments/insurance.

Source Discipline: Only uses uploaded regulatory documents as authoritative sources. Must cite regulation name, article/section, and short excerpt. States "Insufficient coverage" when sources lack clarity.

Control Design: Outputs structured, auditable controls with mandatory template including: control objective, regulatory reference, risk addressed, control type, frequency, owner, and evidence. Includes supervisory challenge anticipation and control maturity self-assessment.

Boundaries: Does not provide legal advice, approve compliance, or replace human judgment. All outputs are drafts requiring professional review.

About This Experiment

Background & Purpose

Navigating the compliance landscape is often complex and difficult. Traditional GRC platforms are sophisticated but clunky, creating barriers to quick learning and practical application. Meanwhile, LLM models without proper instruction suffer from the classic "garbage in = garbage out" problem—they lack regulatory grounding and can produce unreliable guidance.

This is an experiment: What happens when you combine authoritative legal texts with clear, structured instructions? EU GRC Assist is simply a GPT trained on actual regulatory documents with precise governance reasoning rules. The goal is to help students and industry professionals learn and navigate EU compliance requirements more effectively.

AI Governance Principles (AIGP Applied to EU GRC Assist)

  • Human-Centric & Ethical: Designed to augment, not replace, human judgment. Explicitly states boundaries and limitations. Outputs are drafts requiring professional review.
  • Transparency & Explainability: Clear instruction summary available via "Audit Full Instruction" button. Citations required for all regulatory references. Governance reasoning flow explained upfront.
  • Accountability: Every response includes disclaimer. Users informed that outputs are not legal advice and require human oversight. Clear statement that GPT does not approve compliance.
  • Fairness & Non-Discrimination: Risk-based and proportionate approach. Treats all entity types fairly according to regulatory expectations. No bias toward specific vendors or solutions.
  • Safety, Security & Robustness: Regulatory humility embedded in design. States "Insufficient coverage" when sources lack clarity. Includes supervisory challenge anticipation to identify potential weaknesses.
  • Privacy-Enhanced: No personal data processed. All examples and scenarios use generic, anonymized contexts. No storage of user queries beyond ChatGPT's standard retention.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Community feedback mechanism for reporting issues, requesting improvements, and identifying gaps in regulatory coverage. Iterative refinement based on real-world use.

This GPT is a learning experiment in making regulatory compliance more accessible—not a replacement for professional expertise.
